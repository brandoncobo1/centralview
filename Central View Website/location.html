What's Nearby

Vlorë beyond the hotel.

🏖️

Albanian Riviera Beaches

Vlorë is the gateway to the Albanian Riviera — a 100km stretch of hidden coves, turquoise bays, and unspoiled coastline stretching south to Sarandë. Palasa, Drymades, Himara: all within an hour's drive.

🏛️

History & Independence

Vlorë is where Albanian independence was declared in 1912. The Independence Museum, Muradi Mosque (1542), and the ruins of Apollonia (one of antiquity's great Greek cities, 30km away) are unmissable.

🍷

Wine, Food & Local Life

Albania's wine culture is young and exciting. Our team will point you to the best places for fish restaurants on the Lungomare, local raki, and the informal café life that defines Vlorë's afternoons.

Getting Here

How to reach us.

By Car

From Tirana: take the SH4 south through Fier (approx. 2h 30). From the Greek border at Kakavia: 1h 45 north via Gjirokastër. We offer secure on-site parking at €8/night.

By Bus / Furgon

Direct bus connections from Tirana (Kombinat terminal) several times daily, journey approx. 3h. From Greece, cross at Kakavia and take a connection to Vlorë. We can arrange a taxi from the bus station.

Via Corfu

A popular route from Western Europe: fly to Corfu (CFU), take the ferry to Sarandë (35 min), then drive or arrange a transfer north to Vlorë (1h 30). A scenic and efficient option. Ask us for transfer quotes.
