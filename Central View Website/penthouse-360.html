Penthouse — The 360°

Above everything. The Penthouse occupies the entire top floor, its wraparound terrace commanding a cinematic sweep from the Albanian Alps to the open Adriatic. A private plunge pool, a full kitchen, and a living room large enough to host — this is not a hotel room, it is a private residence in the clouds.

All rooms include breakfast prepared fresh each morning on our rooftop terrace — Albanian honey, local cheeses, fresh-baked byrek, and espresso with a view that makes it impossible to rush.
