Privacy Policy

1. Who We Are

The Central View ("we", "us", "our") is a boutique hotel located at Rruga Ismail Qemali, Vlorë 9401, Albania. We are the data controller for personal data collected through this website and in connection with your stay.

For privacy-related enquiries, contact us at: privacy@thecentralview.al

2. Data We Collect

We collect personal data in the following situations:

  • When you make a booking: Name, email address, phone number, arrival/departure dates, number of guests, room preference, and payment information (processed securely by our payment provider — we do not store full card details).
  • When you contact us: Name, email or phone number, and the content of your message (including WhatsApp messages).
  • When you visit our website: IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent (via cookies — see Section 8).
  • During your stay: Passport/ID details as required by Albanian law for guest registration. Special requests or preferences you share with us.

We do not collect sensitive personal data (health information, racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, etc.) unless you voluntarily share it for the purpose of a specific accessibility or dietary need.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data to:

  • Process and manage your reservation
  • Communicate with you before, during, and after your stay
  • Register guests with Albanian authorities as required by law
  • Process payments and prevent fraud
  • Improve our website and services (using aggregated, anonymised analytics)
  • Send you follow-up communications about your stay (where you have given consent or where we have a legitimate interest)

We do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

Where the GDPR applies (e.g. for guests from EEA countries), we process your data on the following legal bases:

  • Contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b)): Processing necessary to fulfil your booking and provide your stay.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): Guest registration with Albanian authorities.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): Fraud prevention, website security, improving our services.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): Marketing communications (where applicable). You can withdraw consent at any time.

5. Who We Share Your Data With

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Payment processors: To handle booking transactions securely (e.g. Stripe or equivalent). These providers are bound by their own privacy policies and applicable law.
  • Albanian authorities: Guest ID data as required by Albanian hospitality law.
  • Service providers: We may use third-party tools for email (e.g. Google Workspace) and website analytics. These providers process data only on our instructions.

All third parties we work with are required to handle your data with appropriate security measures.

6. How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Booking records: 7 years (for accounting and legal compliance).
  • Guest registration data: As required by Albanian law (currently 5 years).
  • Marketing opt-in data: Until you withdraw consent.
  • Website analytics: 26 months (anonymised).
  • Contact enquiries: 2 years from last contact.

7. Your Rights

Under the GDPR and applicable Albanian data protection law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Delete your data ("right to be forgotten"), subject to legal retention requirements
  • Restrict our processing of your data in certain circumstances
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent at any time for processing based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@thecentralview.al. We will respond within 30 days.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Albanian Information and Data Protection Commissioner (IDP) at www.idp.al, or with the supervisory authority in your country of residence.

8. Cookies

Our website uses the following types of cookies:

  • Strictly necessary cookies: Required for the website to function (session management, booking form). These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies: We use anonymised analytics to understand how visitors use our site. No personal data is sent to third parties through these cookies. You can opt out via your browser settings.
  • Preference cookies: Store your language preference. These do not contain personal data.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. We do not run retargeting campaigns.

9. Security

We take data security seriously. We use HTTPS encryption for all data transmission, access controls to limit who can view booking data, and regular review of our data practices.

Payment data is processed by our payment provider using PCI-DSS compliant systems. We never store full card numbers.

In the event of a data breach that affects your rights and freedoms, we will notify you and the relevant supervisory authority as required by law.

10. Contact & Complaints

For any privacy-related questions, to exercise your rights, or to raise a concern:

  • Email: privacy@thecentralview.al
  • Post: The Central View, Rruga Ismail Qemali, Vlorë 9401, Albania
  • WhatsApp: +355 69 123 4567

We aim to resolve all privacy enquiries within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to escalate to the Albanian Information and Data Protection Commissioner.

This policy was last reviewed on 1 January 2025. We may update it periodically. Material changes will be communicated via email to guests who have active bookings at the time of the change.
