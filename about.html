About The Central View

Twenty-one rooms.
One singular view.
Countless reasons to return.

We built The Central View because we believed Vlorë deserved a hotel that matched the drama of its landscape. A place made with patience, not haste. With local hands, not imported formulas.

Our Philosophy

Small on purpose.
Extraordinary
by design.

Twenty-one rooms is a deliberate choice. It means we can know our guests by name. It means breakfast is prepared fresh each morning, not assembled in bulk. It means when you need something, there is a human on the other end — not a system.

We are not a chain hotel that happened to open in Albania. We are Albanian, and we are proud of it. Our design draws from local stone and Adriatic light. Our breakfast table carries honey from Gjirokastra and cheese from the hills behind the city.

The Central View exists at the intersection of international hospitality standards and genuine Albanian warmth. Not one or the other — both, at once.

What We Stand For

Built on three beliefs.

I

Honest Hospitality

We don't perform warmth. We practice it. Our team is trained to listen, to anticipate, and to help — not to upsell. When you ask for a local restaurant recommendation, you'll get the one we'd send a friend to, not the one with a commission.

II

Local First

Our breakfast sourcing is hyperlocal. Our stone is from Albanian quarries. Our artwork is by Albanian artists. We believe travel should leave value in the places it touches — and we put that belief into every purchasing decision.

III

Unhurried Quality

We opened slowly and deliberately. We would rather get the details right than open quickly. A turned-down bed with local lavender. A handwritten note at check-in. A breakfast served when you want it, not when it's convenient for us.

Albania doesn't need more tourism. It needs the right kind. We are trying to be the right kind.
Our Promise

A stay you'll
remember for the
right reasons.

We measure our success not by occupancy rates but by return bookings and by the messages guests send after they leave. By the photographs they share. By the friends they recommend us to.

Vlorë is changing. Albania is opening. We intend to grow with it — carefully, sustainably, and always with the same attention to detail that we started with.
