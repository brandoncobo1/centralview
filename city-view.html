City View Room

Our entry into the sky. Perched above Vlorë's rooftops, the City View Room wraps you in the ambient glow of urban life while the Adriatic shimmers on the horizon. Crisp linens, warm stone textures, and a picture window that frames the city like a living painting.

All rooms include breakfast prepared fresh each morning on our rooftop terrace — Albanian honey, local cheeses, fresh-baked byrek, and espresso with a view that makes it impossible to rush.
